The Work · Three Impressions

Work I

Inking the
Printing Surface

Ink is applied to the press with a hand roller — a brayer. The red-handled tool distributes it evenly across the raised type, a thin film and no more. All of this happens before the paper ever meets the forme.

Work II

Setting Up
the Print

The paper is positioned and the setup checked. The large circular platen is the part that comes down onto the sheet and transfers the inked design. Here the sheet is made ready for its impression.

Work III

Feeding &
Positioning the Paper

A sheet is placed and aligned into the press, squared against the printing area so the design lands exactly where it should. The mechanism is worked by hand, one sheet at a time.
